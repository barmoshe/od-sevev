Monkey Bananas: Support

Last updated 28 September 2026

Something wrong, or an idea for the troop? Write to 1barmoshe1@gmail.com and say which device you play on.

Moving your progress

Settings > Save code > Copy puts your whole save on the clipboard as a text code. On the other device, copy the code and tap Paste. The game asks before it replaces anything.

Nothing happens when I tap

The game counts up to 16 taps a second; faster taps are ignored on purpose, so an auto-clicker gains nothing.

No sound

On iPhone the silent switch mutes the game, like most games. Check Settings > Sound effects and Music too.
