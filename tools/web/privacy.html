Monkey Bananas: Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

Monkey Bananas is a game made by Bar Moshe. This page explains what the game does with your information. The short version: nothing leaves your device.

What the game collects

Nothing. Monkey Bananas has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet while you play.

What the game stores on your device

To keep your troop between sessions, the game saves a few small files inside its own storage on your device (in the browser's storage for the web version):

  • your progress: bananas, producers, upgrades, Thumbs, perks, trophies and stats
  • your settings: sound, music, reduced motion, vibration and number format

These files never leave your device and are not shared with anyone, including me. Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) deletes them.

If you use Settings > Save code > Copy, the game puts a text code of your save on your clipboard. Where you paste it is up to you.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before a version of the game that does anything different is released.
