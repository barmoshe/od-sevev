## Ruling on the Animator's deviation (M1): the roll is accepted

No counter-objection. `ux/mobile-first-layout.md` §5.2 now specifies the roll (D47) and the per-page dwell (D48).
- **The objection is right on the evidence.** A sideways push shows a page's first or last letters at the clip edge for about 300 ms. In Hebrew, ה, ו, ב, ל, ש, מ and כ are one-letter prefixes, so a fragment like "ה" + "כנסת" reads as a word. That breaks "no word is ever cut" on every page change: the rule the pager exists for.
- **The roll cuts glyph *rows*** at the row's top and bottom edges. No reader parses a horizontal slice as a letter. It also keeps every glyph's x, so the eye stays at the right edge where the next line begins.
- **It keeps my intent:** a news-strip move of under 300 ms (240 ms, 80% of it by 100 ms), and still text while reading.
- **Conditions added to §5.2** (they are needed, not optional):
  - a roll always completes; a higher-priority headline waits or cuts, and nothing rests half-rolled;
  - a tap during a roll opens O6 on the **incoming** headline;
  - reduced motion keeps the 200 ms cross-fade.
- **The dwell by length is accepted as specified:** 2.0-5.5 s; ftue 4.5-7.0 s, which keeps my 4.5 s floor.

## Ruling on the pager: the no-break rule (`mobile-first-layout.md` §5.2.1)

A **currency sign stays with what it measures**, and **punctuation stays with its word**. Both are strong glue and never break. A **number stays with its magnitude word** (אלף, מיליון, מיליארד, …). That is weak glue: it breaks only when the whole unit is wider than the line, and then never before ₪.
- **How:** spaces inside a unit become U+00A0 at display time, in `PxText` before shaping and in `Ticker.paginate` before `wrap_lines_px`.
- **The fonts:** all three shipped fonts have U+00A0 at the space's advance, so no budget moves.
- **Measured** on 1,394 content strings: the bad breaks at the 280 / 324 / 384 / 464 clips go from 18 / 20 / 14 / 11 to 1 / 0 / 0 / 0. The one left is "850.6 מיליארד ₪." at 280, which falls back to its weak joint.
- **The Game Designer's lint** checks the strong units at ≤ 280 px.
