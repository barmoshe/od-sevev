## Rules that hold for every post

- **Open as a game or a joke, never as a candidate.** Instagram doesn't recommend political content to
  non-followers by default. So the first frame, the first slide and the first caption line are the game,
  not a name.
- **Hashtags:** game and comedy only. Use #עודסבב #משחק #פיקסלארט #סאטירה #הומור #indiegame #pixelart.
  Don't use election or party hashtags.
- **Test reach with Trial Reels** (they go to non-followers first) before relying on a format.
- **Stories:** 1-3 a day. Reels: 3-5 a week. Carousels and memes fill the gaps.
