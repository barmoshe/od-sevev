## Version page

**Promotional text (170)**
A troop of monkeys builds a civilization for one reason: more bananas. Tap the Big Banana, hire the troop, then evolve into something slightly smarter.

**Description**
Monkey Bananas is a pixel-art idle game about a troop that takes bananas very seriously.

Tap the Big Banana to start. Hire interns, plant trees, send in the hard-hat crew, and let the bureaucrats file every banana in triplicate. Later come catapults, a space program, a time-traveling chimp and a moon that was always a banana.

When the troop is ready, Evolve. You trade the run for Opposable Thumbs, which make every banana count for more, forever. Spend them on perks: fingers that tap on their own, a butler who shops with pocket change, a net for Golden Bananas, longer naps.

- Watch the troop grow on screen as you hire it
- Milestones that double a producer's output as the crew grows
- Trophies that lift the whole troop's morale
- Four eras, from the jungle to banana orbit, each with its own sky and music
- CHIP, the troop's news anchor, reporting every breakthrough
- Keeps harvesting while you are away
- Move your save between devices with a save code
- Reduced motion, number formats and vibration settings
- No ads, no purchases, no account, no tracking. It plays offline.

Made by one developer. Every sprite is drawn in code and every sound is generated in code.
