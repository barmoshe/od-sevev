# Brief addendum, round 2 (from Bar's plan, 28 Sep 2026)

This extends `brief.md`. Where the two disagree, this file wins. Everything under "look" is
**unverified**: check against a photo before final art. Sources are the ones named in the plan;
treat them as the fact sheet for these items.

## Title change
The game is now called **"עוד סבב"** (Another Round). The player character is still
Netanyahu, "הקוסם" (the Magician); only the product title changed. The prestige loop, the
never-ending elections, is now the title.

## Outside-the-box systems (the mechanic is the joke)
- **The coalition as a group chat**: the Coalition tab is a thread **"קואליציה 61 🔒"**. Partners
  post demands as messages with a pay button; unpaid partners "left the group"; poached MKs
  "removed by admin"; Gotliv's transfer reads "X left · added to Otzma". Generic chat styling,
  no WhatsApp branding.
- **The household receipt (main share asset)**: a pixel thermal receipt, "הקיסרות שלך עלתה
  למשפחה הממוצעת ₪___ החודש", with real line items (VAT 18%, fuel ₪8.25/L, electricity and water
  increases, coalition funds) and the countdown to 27.10. Photobombers may appear on it.
- **The Cottage Index**: a pixel cottage-cheese cup in the HUD loses a pixel as your treasury
  grows. (Walla, 1 Jun 2026)
- **"התייעצות ביטחונית"**: the court-day postponement always works, but its cooldown shrinks, its
  cost doubles, and Dubi's excuse gets one sentence longer each time. (Testimony cancelled Apr
  and May 2026.)
- **The departure board**: squeezing the taxpayer and high-tech speeds up a Ben Gurion departure
  board, and prestige never refills it. 69,300 left in 2025 (ToI). The joke is on the policy,
  never on the people leaving.
- **Spin fatigue**: rebuying the same talking point yields less; at high bot-farm levels the
  ticker degrades into word salad.
- **The Suitcase lands on an aide**: catching it pays, but the money lands on an aide's card.
  **"אני לא מכיר אותו"** drops the aide and resets suspicion, at a permanent multiplier cost.
  Qatargate framed exactly as the record: aides indicted/suspected, Netanyahu not a suspect.
- **The real calendar**: HUD counts down to 27.10.2026. From 23.10 no poll-like seat comparison
  ever. After election day the game flips to an endless **"משא ומתן קואליציוני"** mode.
